# PSKY publication failure on 2026-10-06

## Observed failure and issuer identity

Public price run [37541493288](https://github.com/bumblev95/S-P-500-/actions/runs/37541493288)
started at 22:35 UTC after PR #47. Job 112535314434 attempted collection four
times. Each attempt logged `Wrong symbol or failed Nasdaq historical response`
for PSKY, followed by the atomic gate's `PSKY: stale, unfinished or retained
price row; expected 2026-10-06`. No EOD publication checkpoint was produced;
follow-up research run 37543876559 was skipped.

This was a dated corporate-action identity issue, not permission to accept an
older close. [The issuer's October 2 8-K](https://ir.paramount.com/static-files/36b6ca19-67e4-4787-94ee-8c4c1373e0ac)
announced the same Class B common stock would move from Nasdaq/PSKY to
NYSE/SKYD on October 6. [The completed October 6 filing](https://ir.paramount.com/node/73436/html)
identifies Skydance Corporation (CIK 2041610) and its NYSE listing.

Actual responses inspected after the failure, on October 7 UTC:

| Request | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Yahoo PSKY chart | Old Nasdaq metadata; only 7 non-null closes in 49 daily timestamps. By inspection time October 6 Close had been filled, so it cannot serve as the raw failed-run response. |
| Nasdaq PSKY historical | `rCode=400`, code 1001, `Symbol not exists.` |
| Yahoo SKYD chart | NYSE/USD/equity/New York metadata; 2,512 supplied daily closes, including October 6 Close 9.529999732971191. |
| Nasdaq SKYD historical | `rCode=200`, symbol SKYD, October 6 OHLC 9.84/9.84/9.125/9.53. Only the new ticker's first session was supplied. |

Merely changing Nasdaq's URL is insufficient: the old rule requires three
preceding Nasdaq closes, which the new ticker's one-session response lacks.
Yahoo's retired ticker also no longer supplies dependable full history.

## Verified recovery

`scripts/eod_symbol_mapping.py` contains a reviewed, effective-date-bound
PSKY-to-SKYD provider mapping with issuer, security and primary-source references.
The requested logical symbol stays PSKY; `yahooSymbol` records SKYD. No ticker
is guessed from search results, a provider error, a redirect or a matching price.

After the effective date, this route requires a complete Yahoo SKYD daily bar
and the expected symbol, exchange, issuer name, currency, instrument and timezone.
The new history must match the existing published PSKY chart's previous three
closes at cent precision, ending on the immediately preceding completed XNYS
session. Nasdaq's correctly identified SKYD historical response must separately
corroborate the current date and all four OHLC values and supply positive volume.
Nasdaq and Yahoo volume totals need not be identical; this route preserves the
supplied Yahoo OHLCV together and does not mix their volume totals.

The existing missing-Close Nasdaq recovery for ordinary symbols is unchanged.
The new route rejects an incomplete SKYD bar rather than relaxing its existing
three-Nasdaq-anchor rule. It never pads dates, manufactures a bar, merges WBD's
prices into SKYD, or uses an after-hours quote or acquisition consideration.

Evidence includes the exact reviewed mapping, Yahoo metadata/URL, prior
published anchors, complete Nasdaq response/URL/hash and retrieval time. The
collector binds the full exported Yahoo history with SHA-256. The atomic gate
revalidates that evidence, the provider symbol and current OHLCV in CSV, history
and forecast before staging. Missing evidence cannot fall back to a Yahoo-only
source label. The receipt's exact requested symbol set and minimum-count rules
remain intact.

## Independent WBD blocker

[Nasdaq's completed-merger alert](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=ECA2026-710)
states WBD's last trading date was October 5; it was halted before October 6's
open and suspended effective October 7. [WBD's October 6 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1437107/000119312526415055/d200872d8k.htm)
describes cancellation of shares for cash, not a ticker rename into SKYD.

Yahoo currently returns a purported October 6 WBD bar with unchanged
30.950000762939453 OHLC and zero volume; Nasdaq reports N/A volume. This is
not an October 6 exchange close. The collector and publication gate reject
sessions after WBD's documented last trading date. The source constituent CSV
still includes WBD and PSKY, and excludes TWLO and SKYD at inspection time.

[S&P Dow Jones Indices' October 1 announcement](https://press.spglobal.com/2026-10-01-Vylor-Added-to-the-S-P-500-Twilio-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Others-to-Join-S-P-MidCap-400-and-S-P-SmallCap-600)
identifies TWLO as WBD's S&P 500 replacement effective October 6. Replacing a
constituent is a separate universe change, not an alias or a recovered WBD bar.
This patch does not make that change. Genuine 505-symbol production publication
and the downstream research run remain blocked until the requested universe
is reconciled. Reducing the count or accepting WBD's carry-forward would not
meet the requested completed-session condition.

## Regression and verification

`scripts/fixtures/eod-psky-2026-10-06.json` stores the inspected provider responses
(Yahoo metadata plus their last ten bars), original-response hashes, exact URLs
and capture times, and the existing published PSKY anchors. The original
runner's Yahoo response was not retained. Tests explicitly inject its missing
Close to replay the logged failure; they do not claim that injected response
is a raw runner capture. All non-incident stocks and research output in the
505-row local Git replay are synthetic test inputs, not investment evidence.

- The old PSKY route reproduces the Nasdaq rejection and stale-row atomic block.
- The verified route obtains the captured October 6 Close of 9.53, publishes
  all 505 test rows in one commit, restores all exact full-history hashes and
  permits a separate `ml`-only research commit.
- Unknown identities, pre-effective aliases, price-basis mismatches, missing
  anchors, unfinished/missing/duplicate bars, zero volume, missing provenance,
  changed hashes and another stale ticker remain blocked.
- WBD's documented end of trading blocks publication even if its date is
  relabeled and all ordinary CSV/forecast endpoint checks would otherwise agree.

Local checks passed: the 54-test EOD suite plus the added unattested-mapping
regression (55 EOD cases total), 14 forecast tests, 4 price-pipeline tests,
and the existing JavaScript homepage TOP3 and entry-indicator suites. The
original research command order, success/checkpoint gating and exact-artifact
transfer are also covered by the EOD workflow tests. No production research
success is asserted from these tests.

A bounded, read-only publication rehearsal also attempted the actual 505
public tickers with one collection attempt. The first 50 histories were
available; subsequent Yahoo requests were blocked by this execution
environment's network tunnel (`403 Forbidden`, surfaced as `URLError`). The

remote publication was attempted and no research input artifact was produced.
This local network failure is not evidence of a Yahoo rate limit or a
successful production run. Real GitHub EOD/research success still requires
the universe reconciliation and a run from its normal Actions environment.
