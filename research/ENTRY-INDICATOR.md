# Final entry indicator: entry-context-v1

The stock interface now evaluates two coherent entry strategies and a separate
holding response. A high observed-trend score identifies a candidate; it is not
an estimated probability or an instruction to buy. AI validation remains an
independent reference and cannot promote or veto these observed-price rules.

## Entry states

| State | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| 진입 검토 | Uptrend and a valid breakout or confirmed pullback, with acceptable stop risk |
| 눌림목 대기 | Uptrend but price is above the pullback zone; neither setup is ready |
| 돌파 대기 | Uptrend near the previous high, but closing-price breakout is not confirmed |
| 위험 조절 대기 | Breakout or rebound confirmed, but stop risk or pullback reward/risk is insufficient |
| 추격 주의 | Uptrend with excessive distance above the breakout level or prior 20-day mean |
| 지지 회복 대기 | Prior support is broken, or price is below the relevant pullback zone |
| 진입 보류 | Credit risk warning or confirmed medium-term downtrend |
| 관망 | Other mixed/insufficient trend conditions |
| 판단 보류 | Stale/missing/alignment-invalid prices, inadequate history/volatility or unknown market status |

The primary reason and next observation are generated by the same rule that
selects the state. Both strategy cards and five condition checks use the same
plan on the home page, screener and legacy page. Trend-based ranking remains
unchanged and is explicitly labelled as trend ranking.

The beginner view uses a three-category dial (entry withheld, conditions
pending, entry review) with all nine actual states in an icon map. The current
state is marked with a check and outline. Missing data has no dial pointer.
Tapping a state reveals its meaning, not a simulated change to the assessment.
Holding responses use a separate three-state strip. Detailed strategies,
calculations and signal explanations start collapsed; the underlying rules,
score, data and AI validation gates are unchanged.

## Rules and limitations

These constants are design hypotheses, not empirically optimal thresholds.
They must be evaluated on longer, point-in-time data, including costs, multiple
market regimes, historical universe changes and all attempted configurations.
The current 253-bar snapshot is enough to calculate indicators, not to establish
profitability over a representative out-of-sample investment history.

- Uptrend: trend score at least 60, close at/above the 50-day mean and the
  50-day mean at/above the 200-day mean. The existing trend score is unchanged.
- Downtrend: close below the 50-day mean, 50-day mean below the 200-day mean,
  and a negative one-month return. Low score alone is not a sell instruction.
- Breakout: current close exceeds the preceding 55-bar high. With complete
  high/low history this is the actual high; otherwise all 55 observations use
  closes and the card is labelled `55일 종가 돌파`. Never mix the two bases.
- Breakout initial stop: current close minus two observed range units. No
  fixed future profit target or mandatory resistance-based reward/risk gate.
- Volume: current volume divided by the preceding 50-bar mean, with at least
  20 valid observations; otherwise the provider's average-volume input.
  The current bar is excluded from the historical denominator. 1.2x volume is
  confirmation only, not a required Turtle entry condition. Missing volume
  remains missing and the absence of confirmation is visible.
- Pullback: use previous-bar 20/50-day means, a zone of +/-0.5 range units,
  evidence of a nearby support touch in the prior three bars, and a rising
  current close at/above support. The target is the preceding 55-bar high,
  never a fabricated percentage profit or every small historical pivot.
- Pullback stop: the lower of the zone lower edge minus 1.5 range units and
  the prior five-bar low minus 0.25 range units. Reward/risk uses the zone
  upper edge, requiring 1.5x under stable credit or 2x under credit caution.
- Maximum planned price-to-stop risk: 8% under stable credit, 5% under
  caution. These are stock price distances, **not account-equity risk**.
  Breakout risk is from the current close; pullback risk is from the zone top.
  Account size, position sizing and an individual's maximum loss are unknown.
- Extension: above the breakout by more than one range unit or above the
  previous 20-day mean by more than three units. RSI alone does not veto entry.
- Holding: reduce review on confirmed downtrend or a prior 20-day support
  break below the 50-day mean; protect review on other mean/support weakness;
  otherwise trend intact. It does not know cost basis or actual ownership.
  Market/AI failure alone is not turned into a sell instruction.
- All structural levels exclude the current close and future bars. Broken
  prior support is retained, rather than replaced with a lower current level.

`range` is the simple 20-bar mean of actual true ranges only when all required
21 high/low bars are valid. This is not the original Turtle smoothed N estimator.
Older snapshots use annualized close-return volatility / sqrt(252) * price,
explicitly labelled `종가 수익률의 일변동폭`; that proxy is never labelled ATR.
The EOD collector now preserves valid Open/High/Low in the same Yahoo Close
basis (split adjusted, dividends excluded). It never combines dividend-adjusted
Adj Close with other OHLC fields. Forecast publication preserves those bars.
No published prices or AI forecasts were rewritten for this change.

indicator here is an adaptation, not a replication or proof of profitability.

## Validation

The independent, forward-only paper path is documented in
[`simulation/entry-study/README.md`](../simulation/entry-study/README.md).
It freezes this PR's exact rules, first-run universe and settings, keeps the
existing stock account/ledger unchanged, and starts a simultaneous cash control
using the old stock rules. It records signal basis, costs, fills, cancellations
and holding actions in an append-only hash chain. No historical fills or
profitability conclusion are supplied by the regression tests, and there is no
automatic promotion or live-trading connection.

`node scripts/test_entry_indicator.cjs` tests both valid setups, optional/missing
volume, high RSI with moderate price extension, retained broken support,
independent holding responses, credit/data gates, future/malformed/duplicate
bars, price scaling, complete OHLC and explicit close-only fallback.
The existing assessment tests verify all 505 records, both AI horizons,
withheld AI, deterministic ranking, escaping and complete blocker explanations.
Responsive browser QA covers 320/390/1280 px and data failures.
